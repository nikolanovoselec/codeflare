# Frontend design compatibility route

This name remains available for backward compatibility. It owns no separate methodology.

Read [frontend-design](../frontend-design/SKILL.md) and follow it as the canonical frontend art-direction workflow. Classify landing pages and portfolios as **Persuade** or **Experience** surfaces. Classify incumbent work as full redesign, incremental redesign, or polish before choosing the relevant frontend-design reference.

Do not load the retired prescriptive taste manual, apply universal style bans, or combine two art-direction systems. User constraints, product truth, incumbent design commitments, and the selected project-specific visual thesis decide the result.
