# Expressive art object

> Added by Codeflare in 2026 as part of its modified Anthropic canvas-design skill.

Read this for experimental posters, generative artwork, image-led covers, exhibition pieces, and concept-first static compositions.

## Build concept from subject

Find a visual premise specific to the subject, setting, audience, and emotional effect. A manifesto, hidden reference, or abstract system is optional, not required. Direct, literal, documentary, narrative, illustrative, typographic, or playful expression may be stronger.

Define composition, scale, spatial tension, rhythm, palette logic, typography or lettering, image/material language, and one recognizable idea. Originality comes from a coherent response to the brief, not random novelty or mandatory complexity.

## Protect expressive freedom

Text may be absent, sparse, dense, quiet, or dominant when the concept and required content justify it. Overlap, bleed, unusual crops, asymmetry, ornament, repetition, and imperfect material can be intentional. Distinguish deliberate tension from accidental clipping or unreadability.

Do not default to Swiss modernism, Brutalism, scientific notation, clinical labels, thin fonts, geometric patterns, hidden niche references, limited palettes, or “museum quality” rhetoric. Do not dismiss illustration, cartoons, direct communication, or familiar forms as inherently amateur.

## Refine the artifact

Inspect at intended size and viewing distance plus thumbnail scale when relevant. Check whether concept survives without explanation, hierarchy directs attention, typography belongs to composition, material feels intentional, and production defects do not masquerade as expression.

A refinement pass may remove, adjust, or add elements when evidence supports it. Stop when the concept is resolved and requested output is sound; do not polish endlessly.
